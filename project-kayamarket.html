Executive Overview

What is KayaMarket?

KayaMarket is a modern e-commerce marketplace engineered to simplify the buying and selling of authentic food products across Nigeria. The platform bridges the gap between traditional food supply chains and digital consumers, offering a frictionless interface for shoppers while equipping administrators with a centralized control center.

E-Commerce Foodstuff Logistics Direct Delivery
Problem Statement

The Challenge

Purchasing perishable foodstuffs online in emerging markets is frequently hindered by lack of real-time stock clarity, sluggish interfaces, unclear pricing tiers, and complicated checkout flows. The business needed an intuitive, fast-loading digital experience that inspires trust, simplifies high-volume ordering, and works effortlessly on both mobile and desktop.

Stock Visibility Mobile Latency Trust Architecture
Methodology & Strategy

The Approach & Process

Vortex Studio applied a research-led, design-system-first strategy. We created a custom visual language combining appetizing warm tones (vivid Nigerian orange) with clean dark-mode minimalism, ensuring products pop while navigation remains ultra-clean.

Platform Capabilities

UI/UX Architecture & Features

Every screen was engineered for clarity, speed, and conversion efficiency.

Instant Search & Filtering

Category pills, real-time price sliders, and instant keywords allow customers to locate tubers, grains, vegetables, and oils in seconds.

Frictionless Shopping Cart

Persistent slide-over cart with rapid quantity counters, item weight indicators, and direct checkout calculation.

Comprehensive CRM & Admin

Full administrative portal with inventory tracking, order status controls, customer management, and bundle managers.
