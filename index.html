KayaMarket

A Modern Foodstuff Marketplace for Nigeria

KayaMarket is a modern online marketplace designed to simplify the buying and selling of food products across Nigeria. The platform provides an intuitive shopping experience, allowing customers to browse products, place orders, and securely manage their purchases while giving administrators complete control over inventory, categories, products, customers, and content.
